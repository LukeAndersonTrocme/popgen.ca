# Join the Lab

We're building a research group focused on computational and statistical approaches to population genetics. If you're excited about developing methods, working with large datasets, and bridging theory with real-world applications, we'd love to hear from you.

## What We Do

Our work is inherently **interdisciplinary**—we sit at the intersection of genomics, statistics, computer science, and evolutionary biology. Projects in the lab typically involve:

- Developing and implementing statistical methods
- Working with large-scale genomic and genealogical datasets
- Building simulation frameworks
- Creating visualizations and exploratory analyses

This is **methods-oriented research**. We write code, derive models, and run simulations. A typical day might involve debugging an algorithm, running analyses on a computing cluster, or reading papers on spatial statistics.

## Who Should Apply

We welcome students from diverse academic backgrounds. You don't need a biology degree—what matters is curiosity and a willingness to learn. Strong candidates often have:

- Comfort with programming (Python, R, or similar)
- Interest in statistics, probability, or data science
- Enthusiasm for understanding patterns in complex data
- Good communication skills (science is collaborative)

Prior experience in population genetics is helpful but not required. We can teach you the biology.

## Current Opportunities

The lab is in startup mode. If you're interested in potential MSc, PhD, or postdoc positions, reach out to Luke directly to discuss timing and fit.
